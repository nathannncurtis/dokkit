iMessage

iMessages go out through a Mac that runs the dokkit program and is signed in to Messages. No other service is involved.

  1. 1Set it up
  2. 2What arrives
  3. 3Try it

Set it up

On Dokkit's Alerts page, in Settings. Only the owner can change it.

The MacInstall the program on the Mac and enrol it, like any machine, and sign it in to Messages.
dokkit imessage onOn that Mac, run dokkit imessage on so it may send. macOS asks once, on its screen.
Sent fromOn Dokkit's Alerts page, under iMessage, choose that Mac.
Goes toPhone numbers or email addresses, with commas between them.

What arrives

Try it

Under iMessage, press Send a test. It says at once whether it went, or why not.

  • dokkit imessage test <recipient> on the Mac sends one message straight from it, to check Messages itself.
  • Each person can have their own iMessage address, under People.
