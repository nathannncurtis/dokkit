Terms

The rules for the free services dokkit.dev runs for everyone. The software you install has its own licences.

  1. 1Free services
  2. 2Names
  3. 3Fair use

Free services

  • names.dokkit.dev, relay.dokkit.dev and mcp.dokkit.dev are free, run on a small machine, and come with no promise of uptime or support.
  • They may change, be limited or stop. If one is going to stop, this page says so first where that is possible.
  • The software itself is under its own licences: AGPL 3.0 for the server, Apache 2.0 for the program, the libraries and the phone apps.

Names

  • A name is for pointing at a Dokkit server you run. At most 3 names per account.
  • A name lapses when its server stops checking in for 90 days, and is then freed for anyone.
  • Names that impersonate someone, mislead, or are used for phishing, malware or abuse are removed, without notice. Report one at abuse@dokkit.dev.
  • Some names are kept back and cannot be claimed.

Fair use

  • Do not try to get around the limits, overload the services, or use them for anything unlawful.
  • Accounts and mailboxes used that way are removed.

The services are given as they are, without warranty of any kind, and nobody running them is liable for any loss from using them, as far as the law allows.
