Dokkit/Alerts/Email

Email

Email through a mail server you already use, and the daily digest: one email a day with what opened without paging.

  1. 1Set it up
  2. 2What arrives
  3. 3Try it

Set it up

On Dokkit's Alerts page, in Settings. Only the owner can change it.

Mail serverOn Dokkit's Alerts page, under Email: the server, such as smtp.example.com, the port (587 unless told otherwise; 25, 465, 587 and 2525 are allowed), the user name and the password.
Sent fromThe address the mail comes from, such as dokkit@example.com.
Email goes toOne address, or several with commas between them.
Daily digestUnder Limits and night, the time the digest goes out, such as 07:00, in the time zone you choose there.

Try it

Under Email, press Send a test. It says at once whether it went, or why not.

  • Every email is HTML, with a plain copy for mail readers that want one.
  • The digest holds what opened without paging, new errors, and numbers close to a limit. A day with nothing to say sends nothing.
  • Choose Daily digest as a channel for anything that can wait until morning.
